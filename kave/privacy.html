Privacy Policy for Kave

Kave Offline Password Vault · Android app com.aistudio.silovault.k7m9xq
Effective date 27 November 2026 · Last updated 27 November 2026

Short version. Kave stores your encrypted vault on your device. There’s no Kave account, cloud sync, advertising, or analytics. Kave has no Android internet permission. You choose when to copy, fill, or export your data. Google Play handles optional Pro purchases.

1. Your vault

Kave is an Android password manager. It stores the vault entries you add in an encrypted database on your device. This may include logins, notes, payment card details, Wi-Fi credentials, and authenticator secrets. Kave has no app account, cloud vault, ads, or analytics. Kave has no Android internet permission.

2. Features you control

Android Autofill reads supported form fields when you enable Kave as your Autofill provider. Kave sends the selected credential to the requesting app when you approve a fill. If you use a QR scanner, Kave processes camera frames on the device to read the setup code. Android handles biometric verification. Kave stores an encrypted key reference and does not receive the fingerprint or face image.

3. Backups, exports, and clipboard

Kave uses the Android file picker when you import or export a file. You choose the storage provider, which may be a cloud service. Vault backups are encrypted with your backup passphrase. An exported recovery card is readable text and contains your recovery key. Clipboard copies are available to Android and the app where you paste them. Kave requests clipboard clearing after a short delay. Android controls whether clearing can happen while Kave is in the background.

4. Google Play purchases

If you buy Kave Pro, Google Play processes the purchase and provides Kave with a purchase record. Kave uses the record to unlock Pro and stores an encrypted entitlement on your device for offline access. Google’s privacy policy covers Google Play’s handling of your payment and account data.

5. Retention and deletion

Your vault stays on your device until you delete entries, erase your vault in Settings, or remove the app and its data. Erasing the vault leaves your local Pro entitlement available. Files you export remain at the destination you chose until you remove them.
