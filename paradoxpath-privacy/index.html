Privacy Policy for Paradox Path

Paradox Path: Impossible Puzzle · Android game com.stillwavestudio.paradoxpath
Effective date: 20 November 2026 · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Short version. Paradox Path has no sign-up, no accounts and no chat, and we do not run any servers. Your progress is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. Ads come from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID; where the law requires it you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice in the game. The optional Premium purchase is handled by Google Play.

This policy explains what information the Android game Paradox Path ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Stillwave Studio ("we", "us"). By using the game you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in its private storage on your device. We do not receive it:

  • chapters you have finished, stars, coins, relic chests and milestone rewards;
  • your daily puzzle streak and the date you last played the daily path;
  • the wardrobe pieces you own and wear;
  • where you are in an unfinished chapter, so you can continue later;
  • settings such as sound, music and vibration, and which controls the game has already shown you.

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your phone settings, deletes this information.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • Optional videos. You can choose to watch a short video for an extra hint, an extra undo, bonus coins or a wardrobe piece. A video only plays after you tap to watch one.
  • Ads between chapters. None in chapters 1 to 10. After that, at most one after every third chapter, and never within 90 seconds of the previous one.

To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID or other device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data about the ads SDK.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Consent and your ad choices

  • In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads), and manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • Buying Premium removes the ads between chapters. Optional reward videos stay available if you want them.

4. Purchases (Google Play and Unity)

The game offers one optional one-time purchase, Premium (no ads between chapters, 3 hints per chapter, unlimited undo and 8 exclusive wardrobe pieces). Purchases are processed by Google Play; we never see your payment details (Google Payments Privacy Notice). The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing (part of Unity Gaming Services) to talk to Google Play. To run this service Unity may process a random installation ID and basic device and app information (Unity Privacy Policy).

6. Children

Paradox Path is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not run our own servers and hold no data about you. Information on your device stays until you clear the game's data or uninstall it. Data held by Google (advertising, Google Play purchases) is kept according to Google's policies; you can manage it in your Google account at myaccount.google.com. For data held by Unity, see its privacy policy above.

8. Security

Your progress is kept in the game's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The ad and purchase services send their data encrypted (HTTPS). No method of storage is completely secure, but we work to protect the information the game uses.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data on our servers, most requests are handled by clearing the game's data on your device, changing your choice in Settings → Privacy Choices, or through your Google account settings. For anything else, contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and important changes will be announced in the game or on its Google Play page.
