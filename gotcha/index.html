Privacy Policy for Gotcha

Gotcha! Hide & Seek · Android app com.stillwavestudio.gotcha
Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Short version. Gotcha has no account system and we do not run any servers. Your progress is saved on your own device. Local Wi-Fi play only talks to phones on the same network. If ads are shown, they come from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID; you can choose your ad privacy settings in the game and in your phone settings. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

This policy explains what information the mobile game Gotcha ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Stillwave Studio ("we", "us"). By using the game you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device only. We do not receive or store it:

  • Your player name, body colour and chosen cosmetics (hats, trails, frames, emotes).
  • Game progress: level, XP, coins, gems, missions, achievements, career stars, login streak and statistics.
  • Settings such as volume, look speed, graphics and controls.

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, permanently deletes this information.

2. Local Wi-Fi / hotspot multiplayer

When you choose Play with friends, the game finds rooms and plays matches directly between phones on the same Wi-Fi network or phone hotspot. To do this it shares, only with the other players in your room:

  • your player name, body colour and equipped cosmetics;
  • in-game information such as your position, actions and score during the match;
  • your phone's local network address and a 4-digit room code (needed to connect).

This information travels only over your local network. It is not sent to us or stored by us. Other players in the room can see your player name, so please do not use your real full name or personal details as your player name.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game may show ads, including optional rewarded ads (for example "watch an ad to unlock a gadget") and occasional full-screen ads between matches. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version);
  • information about ad interactions (views, clicks, rewards).

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tools. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings (when available) and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads). If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Purchases (Google Play)

If the game offers in-app purchases or a subscription (such as a VIP Pass or gem packs), payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. We receive only the information needed to confirm and restore your purchase, such as a purchase token and the product you bought. Google's handling of payment information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Google Play Games (optional)

If the game offers Google Play Games sign-in and you choose to use it, Google provides the game with your Play Games profile (player ID, display name, avatar) so it can offer achievements, leaderboards and cloud save. Cloud save stores your game progress in your Google account. You can play without signing in.

6. Game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may process limited technical information (such as device model and operating system version) as described in the Unity Privacy Policy. We have not enabled Unity's analytics or other online services in the game.

8. Children

Gotcha is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and deletion

We do not keep any of your game data on our own systems. Data on your device stays until you delete the app or clear its data. Data held by Google (advertising, Google Play, Play Games) is kept according to Google's policies; you can manage it in your Google account at myaccount.google.com.

10. Security

Your save file is stored in the app's private storage and protected against tampering. Local multiplayer only works between phones on the same network. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect the information the game uses.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data on our servers, most requests are handled by deleting the app's data on your device or through your Google account settings. For anything else, contact us.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes (for example when new features are added). The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and important changes will be announced in the game or on its Google Play page.
