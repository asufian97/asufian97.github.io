About this policy

This policy describes the current ad-free version of Water Sort Puzzle, developed by Stillwave Studio, and how to contact us about privacy.

Game data

The game does not require an account. Puzzle progress, saved boards, best move counts, daily completion, and audio preferences are stored locally on your device to provide gameplay and resume functions. We do not receive those gameplay records on a developer-operated server.

The current version includes no advertising, in-app purchases, or developer analytics services. Gameplay does not require access to contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Retention and control

Local progress is retained until you clear application storage or remove the app. Your operating system may back up and restore application data according to your device settings. Clearing application storage resets saved puzzles and preferences. There is no game account to delete.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your support or privacy request and keep correspondence only as needed to resolve it and meet applicable obligations. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or identity documents.

Store, device, and website services

Google Play, your operating system, and your email provider operate their own services under their respective privacy policies. Visiting this privacy page also involves its hosting provider, GitHub Pages. These providers' services are separate from local game progress storage.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Future changes

If a future version adds advertising, analytics, accounts, or other services, we will update this policy and applicable in-app disclosures to explain their data practices before those features are released. The updated date will appear on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests about support correspondence, contact Stillwave Studio at sufianwp97dot@gmail.com.
