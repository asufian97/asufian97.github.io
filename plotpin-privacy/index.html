Privacy Policy for PlotPin

PlotPin: GPS Land Area Measure · Android app com.stillwavestudio.gpslandareameasure
Effective date: 24 November 2026 · Last updated: 24 November 2026

Short version. PlotPin has no sign-up and no accounts, shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking SDK, and we do not run any servers. Your measured plots, coordinates, notes and photos are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. The map is provided by Google Maps and place-name search uses Google's geocoding service, so those features send some data to Google and need an internet connection. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

This policy explains what information the Android app PlotPin ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by Stillwave Studio ("we", "us"). By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Location

PlotPin asks for your device's precise and approximate location only after you tap My Location or Walk GPS, and only if you agree. Location is used to:

  • centre the map on where you are;
  • record boundary corners while you walk around a plot with Walk GPS;
  • show GPS accuracy so you can decide when to mark a corner.

While Walk GPS is running, the app keeps tracking with the screen off using a foreground service and shows a notification ("Walking boundary") the whole time. Tracking stops when you finish the walk or close the app. PlotPin does not track your location in the background at other times and does not request background location permission.

Coordinates you record become part of the plots you save on your device. We never receive your location. You can withdraw location permission at any time in your phone's settings; you can still measure by placing points on the map by hand.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in its private storage on your device. We do not receive it:

  • saved plots: names, boundary coordinates, area and perimeter, colours, folders and field notes;
  • photos you attach to a plot, either chosen from your gallery with Android's photo picker or taken with your camera app (PlotPin copies the photo into its private storage; it does not access the rest of your gallery);
  • settings such as units, custom unit ratios, map layer, theme and a PDF report header you enter.

Android may include this app data in your personal device backup to your Google account if you have backup turned on in your phone's settings. That backup is handled by Google, not by us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your phone settings deletes the information on the device.

3. Google Maps and place search

The map in PlotPin is provided by the Google Maps SDK for Android. To load map imagery and run the map, Google may collect information such as your IP address, device and app information, map interactions, and, when the "my location" dot is shown, your device location. Google uses this according to its own policies.

If you search for a place name, the text you type is sent to Google's geocoding service through Android to find the coordinates. Searches for decimal, DMS or UTM coordinates are processed on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Google Maps terms: maps.google.com/help/terms_maps · Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Sharing, export and import

PlotPin only shares data when you ask it to. When you share a plot card or export a PDF report, KML, GeoJSON, GPX or CSV file, the app creates the file on your device and hands it to the app you pick in Android's share sheet (for example email or a messaging app). What happens next is governed by that app. When you import a file, PlotPin reads only the file you select.

5. Purchases (Google Play)

PlotPin Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. The app receives only what it needs to confirm and restore your purchase, such as the product you bought and a purchase token. Google's handling of payment information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. No ads, no analytics

PlotPin shows no advertising and does not use the advertising ID. It contains no analytics or tracking SDK. If the app crashes, Google Play may send us anonymous technical crash reports (device model, Android version, the error), as described in Google's policies.

8. Children

PlotPin is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and deletion

We do not run our own servers and hold no data about you. Information on your device stays until you delete plots in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Data held by Google (Maps, Google Play purchases, device backups, crash reports) is kept according to Google's policies; you can manage it in your Google account at myaccount.google.com.

10. Security

Your plots, notes and photos are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. No method of storage is completely secure, but we work to protect the information the app uses.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data on our servers, most requests are handled by deleting plots or clearing the app's data on your device, withdrawing the permissions above, or through your Google account settings. For anything else, contact us.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes (for example when new features are added). The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and important changes will be announced in the app or on its Google Play page.
