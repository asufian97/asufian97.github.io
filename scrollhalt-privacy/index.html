Privacy Policy for ScrollHalt

ScrollHalt: Reels & Alert Lock · Android app com.stillwavestudio.scrollhalt
Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Short version. ScrollHalt has no sign-up and no accounts, and we do not run any servers. Everything ScrollHalt learns about how you use your phone (which apps you open, the Reels or Shorts it stops, the notifications it holds) is processed and stored only on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Ads for free users come from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID; you can choose your ad privacy settings in the app. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

This policy explains what information the Android app ScrollHalt ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by Stillwave Studio ("we", "us"). By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Accessibility Service (Reels Guard)

ScrollHalt's core features use Android's AccessibilityService API. The app asks for this permission only after showing an in-app explanation, and only if you agree. It is used to:

  • detect when you open an app you chose to guard, so ScrollHalt can show a mindful pause screen;
  • detect short-form video players (such as Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts) inside apps you chose, so ScrollHalt can back out of them while the rest of the app (messages, search, feed) stays usable;
  • return you to the Home screen when a session time you chose runs out.

To do this the app looks at the name of the app in the foreground and at on-screen element IDs and labels in guarded apps, only to recognise video players. ScrollHalt does not read your messages, passwords or what you type, does not record your screen, and does not collect personal content. All of this processing happens on your device. No accessibility data is stored externally, shared, or transmitted over the network. The app records only a local log of each pause or block (time, app name and type) so it can show your statistics.

You can turn the service off at any time in Settings → Accessibility on your phone.

2. Notification access (Alert Vault)

If you turn on the optional Alert Vault (a Pro feature), ScrollHalt asks for notification access, again after an in-app explanation. While a focus session or quiet hours are active, it holds non-urgent notifications from apps you chose. For each held notification it saves, on your device only:

  • the sending app's name;
  • the notification title and a short preview of its text;
  • the time it arrived.

Phone calls, alarms, media controls, ongoing notifications and apps you mark as "Always Allow" are never held. Held notifications are never sent off your device. Previews older than 30 days are deleted automatically, and you can clear the Vault at any time in the app. You can withdraw notification access at any time in your phone's settings.

3. Other information stored on your device

The app also saves the following in its private storage on your device. We do not receive it:

  • the apps you chose to protect and their settings (full pause, Reels-only, Strict Mode, Always Allow);
  • a local activity log: time saved estimates, number of pauses and blocks, focus sessions and schedules;
  • settings such as digest times and whether the shield is on.

To let you pick apps, ScrollHalt reads the list of apps on your phone that have a launcher icon, and their names and icons. This list stays on your device. The app's data is excluded from cloud backups. Uninstalling the app, using Clear Local Logs & Vault in the app, or clearing the app's data in your phone settings deletes this information.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

For free users the app shows a banner ad on the main screen and offers optional rewarded ads (watch videos to unlock a free 24-hour Pro Pass). The pause screen never shows ads. Pro users do not see ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version);
  • information about ad interactions (views, clicks, rewards).

ScrollHalt never gives advertisers any of the on-device information described in sections 1–3.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tools. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad Consent & Privacy Options in the app, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads). If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Purchases (Google Play)

ScrollHalt Pro is offered as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. The app receives only what it needs to confirm and restore your purchase, such as the product you bought and a purchase token, and it checks Google's signature on your device. Google's handling of payment information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel a subscription in the Google Play Store.

6. Crash reports

If the app crashes, Google Play may send us anonymous technical crash reports (device model, Android version, the error), as described in Google's policies. The app has no analytics or tracking SDK of its own.

8. Children

ScrollHalt is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and deletion

We do not run our own servers and hold no data about you. Information on your device stays until you clear it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app; held notification previews are removed automatically after 30 days. Data held by Google (advertising, Google Play purchases, crash reports) is kept according to Google's policies; you can manage it in your Google account at myaccount.google.com.

10. Security

Your rules, activity log and held notifications are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and are excluded from cloud backups. No method of storage is completely secure, but we work to protect the information the app uses.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data on our servers, most requests are handled by clearing the app's data on your device, withdrawing the permissions above, or through your Google account settings. For anything else, contact us.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes (for example when new features are added). The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and important changes will be announced in the app or on its Google Play page.
